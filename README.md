## What Black Umbrella Actually Does

At its core, Black Umbrella is an AI marketing platform that generates complete ad campaigns — copy, visuals, hooks, CTAs — fast. We're talking 45 to 150 ready-to-publish Meta ad variations in under 15 minutes. Not rough drafts. Not placeholder text you have to rewrite. Actual campaign-ready creatives.

The whole thing is built around a suite of tools that work together:

**Ask Astro AI** is the brain. It's a collection of 15+ specialized AI agents that function like a marketing team on demand. Stuck on a campaign angle? Ask Astro. Need to optimize ad copy? Ask Astro. Want a full campaign strategy? Astro's got it. The pitch is essentially "a CMO in your pocket, available at 3am."

**Brand Hub** is where you store your brand's DNA — colors, voice, tone, guidelines. Once it's in there, every campaign Astro generates stays on-brand automatically. If you're managing multiple clients or brands, this alone saves hours of briefing time.

**Ads Generator** is the headline feature. Pick your objective (lead gen, sales, ecommerce), load your brand, customize your preferences, and Astro generates complete ad packages — including AI-created images using Flux models, not stock photos. Everything is pre-checked against Meta's advertising policies.

**Ad Variations Tester** helps you find winners roughly 3x faster than manual testing by systematically running combinations.

**Meta Bulk Publisher** lets you push up to 1,000 ad variations to Meta Ads Manager in a few clicks. That's the kind of thing that used to take an entire afternoon.

**Shareable Campaigns** generates secure view-only links for client approvals — no more email chains with attached PDFs that nobody can find later.
